# Commercial terms — decisions required before publication

The seller is Aivana GmbH. This development document is a review checklist, not a binding offer or approved license agreement.

The commercial proposal is a per-user monthly subscription. The price, currency, inclusive/exclusive tax treatment, territory availability, trial policy, renewal terms, cancellation timing and refund policy require seller approval. Stripe Price and Customer Portal configuration must agree with the approved terms.

Define the licensed user and permitted installations, supported Windows/protocol versions, entitlement verification requirements and service availability. The current integration verifies online subscription evidence and does not promise indefinite offline licensing. Development access is not a commercial entitlement.

Define acceptable use, authorization requirements for remote hosts, responsibility for backups and recovery approvals, confidentiality, data-processing roles, support scope, statutory rights, warranty/liability terms and governing law through qualified review. Avoid promising universal recovery, complete redaction, outage prediction or measured labor savings: the implemented evidence does not establish those claims.

Publish approved terms and privacy information through the seller-controlled site and the Stripe checkout configuration before enabling live commerce. Approval must come from the seller; generated draft text and a configuration flag cannot supply it.
